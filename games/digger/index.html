Help

Dig

Tap the shaft to dig deeper. You earn dirt as you go — spend it in the Shop .

Shop

Open panels with the menu (or the tabs on wide screens). Per tap upgrades make each click dig more. Auto dig keeps digging while you watch (or while you are away). Use Pause / Resume on Auto dig when you need a break.

Dirt coins

Bronze coins appear in the shaft. Tap them before you dig past for a bonus. Deeper coins pay more; chaining pickups builds a combo. Pause auto dig if you need time to grab them.

Side mineshafts

Abandoned tunnels open in the cutaway walls as you dig — rails, cobwebs, and torches. Tap a minecart for a large dirt bonus, a barrel for dirt plus a short auto-dig burst, or a crate for +1 level on a random Per tap tool you already own. Looted props stay emptied.

Finds & special coins

Discoveries unlock as you dig — browse them under Finds . Rarer special coins are collectibles with their own tap bonus.

Goals

Achievements celebrate milestones. They do not boost dig power — just the thrill of the descent. A toast pops when you unlock one.

Layers

Deeper rock is harder. Your dig rates slow as you enter new layers — keep upgrading to push through.

Offline

If you own auto dig and step away for a while, claim a reduced offline reward when you return (capped at 24 hours).
